# Privacy Policy — Project n8n

_Last updated: November 5, 2026_

## What this app is

Project n8n is a personal, self-hosted automation run by Jaison Kurian for his own use only. It connects to his own Gmail account so that auction invoice emails he receives can be filed automatically into his personal records on his own home server. The app has no other users, is not offered to the public, and is not a commercial service.

## What data the app accesses

Through Google OAuth, the app accesses the owner's own Gmail messages and attachments — specifically, emails from auction houses that contain invoice PDFs — so the automation can save the invoice details (seller, lot numbers, prices, totals) into the owner's personal tracking spreadsheet/database.

## How the data is used

- Data is processed only on the owner's own home server, which he owns and controls.
- Invoice details are stored only in the owner's personal records on that server.
- Data is **never** sold, rented, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train models.

## Data retention and control

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from his Google Account (Security → Third-party access), which immediately stops the automation from reading any further mail. Stored invoice records remain only on his own server, under his control, and he can delete them at any time.
